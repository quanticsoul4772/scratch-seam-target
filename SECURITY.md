# Security policy for scratch-seam-target

Thanks for looking out for this repo. This is a small scratch/pilot repo — it
contains only documentation files and no running code — but security reports
are still handled seriously, and the process is deliberately simple.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities privately by email:

- **Contact:** `security@REPLACE-WITH-OPERATOR-EMAIL.example`
  (placeholder — to be filled in by the repository operator)

Please do **not** report security issues through the public issue tracker,
in pull requests, or in any other public channel. Public reports can expose
a problem before there is a chance to address it.

When reporting, include enough detail to reproduce or understand the issue:
what you found, where you found it, and why you believe it is a security
problem.

## How reports are handled

- Your report will be acknowledged within 5 business days.
- The report will be reviewed to confirm the issue and assess its impact.
- You'll be kept informed of progress until the issue is resolved or a
  decision is made, and you'll be told the outcome either way.
- Please keep the details of your report private until a fix or response
  is in place — good-faith, coordinated disclosure is expected on both sides.

There is no bug bounty program for this repo. Given its scratch/pilot nature,
most reports are likely to concern documentation accuracy or repository
configuration rather than exploitable code, but all reports made in good
faith are welcome.

This policy is still being established and may change as the repo grows.
